# Browsify Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-07-18

Browsify collects no data whatsoever.
It has no analytics and makes no network requests.
All settings are stored locally in macOS user defaults.
URLs are processed on-device only and passed directly to the browser or app you choose.
Browsify does not transmit, sell, or share your information.
For questions, contact us through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/izyuumi/Browsify/issues).
