# Browsify Website

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Mac users who keep work, personal, testing, and meeting links in different browsers, browser profiles, or desktop apps. They want links to land correctly without copying URLs or thinking about routing each time.

## Product Purpose

Explain Browsify quickly, show the real Mac app, and give visitors a direct path to the Mac App Store. Success means a visitor understands that Browsify routes links locally using their rules and can confidently install it.

## Positioning

Every link opens in the browser, profile, or app where it belongs.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: View Browsify on the Mac App Store.
- Secondary CTA: See the real app interface and read its privacy policy.
- Ten-second takeaway: Route every link to the right place.
- Belief ladder: Browsify removes link-copying friction; rules and a fast picker cover both predictable and unexpected links; the app works locally without accounts, analytics, or an online service.
- Proof on hand: Real App Store screenshots in `../Store/`; source-backed privacy policy in `../PRIVACY.md`.

## Brand Personality

Precise, native, quietly vivid. The site should feel like a focused Mac utility: direct language, confident spacing, and a small amount of energetic cyan and violet.

## Anti-references

No generic SaaS card grid, glassmorphism, gradient text, fake metrics, testimonials, inflated claims, or developer-terminal styling. Avoid ornamental complexity that competes with the app.

## Design Principles

1. Show the real product before explaining every capability.
2. Turn routing into a simple, legible visual story.
3. Keep claims exact and verifiable from the app and repository.
4. Use the icon palette as identity, not decoration.
5. Make installation and support paths obvious.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast, preserve keyboard-visible focus, support 320px-wide screens, provide useful image alternatives, and remove nonessential motion when reduced motion is requested.
