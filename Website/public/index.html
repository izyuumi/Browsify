Every link. Right place.

Browsify sends links to the browser, profile, or Mac app where they belong. Rules handle the routine. A fast picker handles the rest.

Links stop being little decisions.

Browsify sits between a click and its destination. Match a rule, or choose with one key.

  1. You click a linkFrom Mail, Messages, Slack, or any other app.
  2. →
  3. Browsify checks rulesMatch a domain, URL pattern, or source app.
  4. →
  5. It opens in contextBrowser, profile, desktop app—or your picker.

When no rule matches, pick without slowing down.

The compact browser picker keeps the URL visible and puts every destination on a keyboard shortcut.

  • Customize picker keys with letters, numbers, symbols, arrows, or function keys.
  • Reorder browsers, hide the ones you do not use, and add custom browsers.
  • Target Chrome, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi, or Firefox profiles when you grant access.

Your links stay on your Mac.

Browsify has no accounts, analytics, or online service. It makes no network requests. URLs are processed on-device and settings stay in macOS user defaults.
